## About Me

I am a Computer Science Master's student interested in **Artificial Intelligence,
Data Science, and Environmental AI**.

My current work focuses particularly on **drought and climate forecasting**,
time-series modeling, machine learning, and deep learning. I also explore
Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision through practical,
research-oriented projects.

I am interested in developing AI methods that are not only accurate, but also
reproducible, interpretable, and useful for real-world scientific applications.
